          How to play

          1. Leave your land to draw a trail.
          2. Get back home to claim everything inside the loop.
          3. Touch another player's trail to knock them out.
          4. If anyone touches your trail, or you cross it yourself, you're out.
          5. Head-on crash? The shorter trail wins.
          6. Power-ups: ⚡ speed, 🛡 shield for your trail, 💣 instant land.
